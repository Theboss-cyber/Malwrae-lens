❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my uploaded file executed?

No. MalwareLens is a static analyzer — files are inspected without ever being run. This makes it safe to check suspicious samples.

How does the risk score work?

Every finding (entropy, suspicious strings, imports, signatures, VirusTotal) contributes to a transparent 0–100 score with readable reasoning behind it.

Are my reports private?

Yes. Reports are scoped to your account only and stored temporarily on the server. On the free tier, report history is rebuilt from a local store.

Can the tool harm my computer?

No. Uploads live in an isolated temporary folder, are never executed, and are periodically deleted. Only upload samples you have permission to examine.

Why is VirusTotal grayed out?

The integration needs an API key configured by the operator. Without one, MalwareLens still works fully offline using its own analyzers.
